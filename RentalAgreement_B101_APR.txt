TERMS AND CONDITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. TENANCY PERIOD
   The tenancy shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months, commencing from 15th April 2026 and ending on 14th March 2027, unless renewed or terminated as per this Agreement.

2. RENT PAYMENT
   a. The Lessee shall pay the monthly rent of ₹75,000/- on or before the 5th day of every calendar month, in advance, via bank transfer/UPI to the Lessor's designated account.
   b. Delay beyond 7 days from the due date shall attract a late fee of ₹200/- per day, capped at ₹3,000/- per month.

3. RENT ESCALATION
   Upon renewal, the monthly rent may be increased by a maximum of 5% (five percent) per annum, subject to mutual agreement between both parties.

4. MAINTENANCE CHARGES
   a. The Lessee shall bear the maintenance charges as fixed by the APR Residents Welfare Association.
   b. Any increase in maintenance charges during the tenancy period shall be shared equally (50:50) between the Lessor and the Lessee, unless the increase is below 10% of the current charges, in which case the Lessee shall bear it fully.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
   a. The total security deposit is ₹3,00,000/- (inclusive of the ₹25,000/- token amount already paid).
   b. The security deposit is interest-free and refundable.
   c. The Lessor shall refund the security deposit within 15 (fifteen) days of the Lessee vacating and handing over the premises, after deducting any legitimate dues as specified in this Agreement.
   d. If the Lessor fails to refund the deposit within 15 days, simple interest at 9% per annum shall accrue on the outstanding deposit amount from the 16th day until the date of actual refund.

6. NOTICE PERIOD AND TERMINATION
   a. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 1 (one) month's written notice to the other party.
   b. The notice period applies equally to both the Lessor and the Lessee.
   c. If the Lessee vacates after serving the full 1-month notice period, no rent deduction shall be made from the security deposit, regardless of whether a replacement tenant is found.
   d. If the Lessee vacates WITHOUT serving the full notice period, rent for the shortfall period shall be deducted from the deposit.
   e. If the Lessor terminates the Agreement before the 11-month period without the Lessee being in breach, the Lessor shall pay the Lessee a compensation of 1 (one) month's rent in addition to refunding the full security deposit within 7 days.

7. LOCK-IN PERIOD
   There is no lock-in period. Either party may terminate with 1 month's notice as stated in Clause 6.

8. UTILITY CHARGES
   The Lessee shall pay all utility charges including but not limited to electricity, water (if metered separately), internet/broadband, DTH/cable, and gas, directly to the concerned service providers on or before their due dates.

9. USE OF PREMISES
   a. The premises shall be used strictly for residential purposes only.
   b. The maximum number of occupants shall not exceed 4 (four) at any time.
   c. The Lessee shall not sublet, sub-lease, or assign the premises or any part thereof to any third party without written consent of the Lessor.

10. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
    a. Minor repairs and day-to-day maintenance (up to ₹5,000/- per instance) shall be borne by the Lessee.
    b. Major repairs, structural repairs, plumbing/electrical line repairs, and repairs due to normal wear and tear exceeding ₹5,000/- shall be the responsibility of the Lessor.
    c. The Lessor shall attend to major repair requests within 7 days of being notified. If the Lessor fails to do so, the Lessee may get the repairs done and deduct the cost from the next month's rent, with prior written intimation and supporting bills.

11. DAMAGE TO PROPERTY
    a. The Lessee shall maintain the premises and all fixtures in good condition, subject to normal wear and tear.
    b. Any damage beyond normal wear and tear, caused by the Lessee, shall be deducted from the security deposit at actuals, supported by bills/estimates.
    c. Normal wear and tear includes minor scuff marks on walls, fading of paint due to sunlight, and minor wear on fixtures due to regular use.

12. PAINTING AND REPAINTING
    a. If the tenancy lasts 11 months or less, no painting charges shall be applicable, as minor wall wear is considered normal wear and tear.
    b. If the tenancy extends beyond 11 months (via renewal), painting charges at the time of vacating shall be shared equally (50:50) between the Lessor and Lessee, at actuals, supported by quotations/bills.
    c. In no case shall painting charges exceed the actual cost of painting. Flat-rate deductions (e.g., "1 month rent for painting") shall not apply.

13. INSPECTION
    The Lessor or their authorized representative may inspect the premises at a mutually convenient time, with at least 24 hours' prior notice to the Lessee. The Lessor shall not make unannounced visits.

14. SOCIETY RULES
    The Lessee shall abide by the rules and regulations of the APR Residents Welfare Association and shall not cause any disturbance to neighbours or common areas.

15. HANDOVER ON VACATING
    a. Upon vacating, the Lessee shall hand over the premises in the same condition as received, subject to normal wear and tear.
    b. The Lessee shall obtain a "No Dues Certificate" from the APR Residents Welfare Association.
    c. Both parties shall jointly inspect the premises on the date of vacating and prepare a handover checklist.

16. DEFAULT IN RENT
    If the Lessee defaults on rent for 2 (two) consecutive months, the Lessor may terminate this Agreement with 15 days' written notice.

17. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
    a. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be resolved amicably through mutual discussion.
    b. If unresolved, the dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator mutually appointed, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
    c. The jurisdiction for any legal proceedings shall be the courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka.

18. GOVERNING LAW
    This Agreement shall be governed by the Karnataka Rent Control Act and the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and other applicable laws of India.

19. RENEWAL
    The Lessee shall have the first right to renew this Agreement upon expiry, subject to mutually agreed revised terms. The Lessor shall give the Lessee at least 30 days' notice if they do not intend to renew.
